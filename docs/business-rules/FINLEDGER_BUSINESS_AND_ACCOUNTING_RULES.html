1. Executive Accounting Charter

FinLedger serves as the core financial system of record for KVJ Analytics. This document specifies the immutable business, accounting, tax, workflow, and navigation rules governing the application. Every ledger voucher, financial statement, and transaction workflow must conform to these rules without deviation.

Section A1: General Accounting Rules

RULE-GEN-001 Pure Double-Entry Bookkeeping TEST-GEN-001

FinLedger strictly enforces double-entry accounting. For every financial event, total debits must exactly equal total credits (Σ Dr = Σ Cr). Unequal entries are mathematically blocked.

Module: services/accounting-engine.service.ts, app/(dashboard)/journals

RULE-GEN-002 Accounting Engine as Single Source of Truth TEST-GEN-002

The Accounting Engine (AccountingEngine) and the General Ledger are the sole authoritative sources for all accounting balances, statuses, and reports. Module-level summary caches cannot override the GL.

Module: services/accounting-engine.service.ts, app/(dashboard)/ledgers

RULE-GEN-003 Absolute Balance Equilibrium TEST-GEN-003

Every posted journal entry validates balance integrity prior to ledger persistence. Suspense balance or artificial adjustments are strictly prohibited.

Module: services/accounting-engine.service.ts, app/(dashboard)/reports/trial-balance

RULE-GEN-004 Five-Fold Account Classification TEST-GEN-004

Every ledger account belongs to one of five core classifications: Asset, Liability, Equity, Income, or Expense. GST and TDS are explicitly segregated into statutory balance sheet heads.

Module: services/chart-of-accounts.service.ts, app/(dashboard)/masters

RULE-GEN-005 Non-Duplication on Settlement TEST-GEN-005

Payments and receipts are balance sheet settlements; they never create secondary income or expense entries upon disbursement or receipt.

Module: services/tax-invoice.service.ts, services/expense.service.ts

Section A2: Sales & Customer Invoices

RULE-SALES-001 Revenue Recognition TEST-SALES-001

Service value (taxable amount) is recognized as operating income upon confirmation of a Tax Invoice, irrespective of customer payment status.

Debit: Accounts Receivable (Customer) ₹11,800
Credit: Revenue from Operations (Income) ₹10,000
Credit: Output GST Payable (Liability) ₹1,800
Module: services/tax-invoice.service.ts, app/(dashboard)/invoices

RULE-SALES-002 Separation of GST from Revenue TEST-SALES-002

GST collected on behalf of the Government is a statutory balance sheet liability and is strictly excluded from P&L revenue.

Module: services/accounting-engine.service.ts

RULE-SALES-009 Distinction: Proforma vs. Tax Invoice TEST-SALES-009

Proforma Invoices are commercial quotations with zero accounting or tax impact. Only confirmed Tax Invoices generate journal vouchers and tax liabilities.

Module: app/(dashboard)/proforma-invoices, services/proforma-invoice.service.ts

Section A3: Customer Receipts & Customer TDS

RULE-RCPT-009 Customer TDS Recognized on Actual Deduction TEST-RCPT-009

Customer TDS is recognized as TDS Receivable (Current Asset) strictly when the customer actually deducts it upon settlement, never at invoice issuance.

Debit: Bank Account ₹10,800
Debit: TDS Receivable (Current Asset) ₹1,000
Credit: Accounts Receivable (Customer) ₹11,800
(Operating Revenue remains unchanged at ₹10,000)
Module: services/tax-invoice.service.ts, services/accounting-engine.service.ts

RULE-RCPT-004 Derived Invoice Payment Statuses TEST-RCPT-004

Invoice payment status is strictly derived: Not Paid (₹0 paid), Partially Paid (0 < paid < gross), and Paid (paid ≥ gross).

Module: services/tax-invoice.service.ts

Sections A4 & A5: Expenses & Payment Modes

RULE-MOD-001 Strict Two-Mode Enforcement TEST-MOD-001

Only two user-facing payment modes are permitted: KVJ Paid (company bank/cash account) and Employee Paid (paid personally by staff). No separate "Payable Later" mode exists.

Module: app/(dashboard)/expenses/new, services/expense.service.ts

RULE-MOD-004 Employee Paid Expense Workflow TEST-MOD-004

Employee Paid expenses recognize the business expense immediately and credit Employee Payable liability. Bank/Cash accounts are untouched until reimbursement.

At Expense Incurrence:
Debit: Business Expense ₹5,000
Credit: Employee Payable (Liability) ₹5,000

At Reimbursement:
Debit: Employee Payable (Liability) ₹5,000
Credit: Bank Account ₹5,000
Module: services/expense.service.ts, services/accounting-engine.service.ts

Section A6: Employees & Salary

RULE-EMP-001 Strict Segregation of Employees from Vendors TEST-EMP-001

Employees are distinct internal master records, completely separate from commercial vendors. They must never be classified as Trade Creditors (Sundry Creditors).

Module: services/employee.service.ts, app/(dashboard)/masters

RULE-EMP-009 Mandatory Working "Add Employee" Option TEST-EMP-009

The application must provide a functional, accessible option to add, edit, and select employees across Masters and Expense workflows.

Module: app/(dashboard)/masters, app/(dashboard)/expenses

Section A8: GST & Input Tax Credit (ITC)

RULE-GST-002 Intra-State vs. Inter-State Determination TEST-GST-002

Kerala (State Code 32) to Kerala transactions charge CGST + SGST. Inter-state transactions charge IGST. Export transactions to overseas clients (B2B_EXPORT) are zero-rated.

Module: lib/constants/indian-states.ts, services/tax-invoice.service.ts

RULE-GST-006 Eligible ITC Excluded from Cost TEST-GST-006

Eligible Input GST is debited to Input Tax Credit Asset; it is strictly excluded from P&L expense cost. Ineligible ITC is capitalized into expense cost.

Module: services/accounting-engine.service.ts

Sections A11 & A12: Reports & Frozen Navigation Model

RULE-RPT-003 Trial Balance Equilibrium TEST-RPT-003

Total Debits must equal Total Credits exactly (Δ = 0.00) across any period.

Module: app/(dashboard)/reports/trial-balance

RULE-RPT-004 Schedule III Balance Sheet Equilibrium TEST-RPT-004

Assets = Liabilities + Equity exactly for every reporting period.

Module: app/(dashboard)/reports/balance-sheet

RULE-NAV-001 Frozen 11-Item Sidebar Navigation TEST-NAV-001

The standard left sidebar contains exactly 11 modules: Dashboard, Ledgers, Journal, Invoice, Expense, Banking & Cash, Masters, Users, Reports, Opening/Closing, Settings.

Module: components/Sidebar.tsx
